Cricket Pulse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

What we collect

The Android app works without an account. We may process standard technical logs on our API (needed to run and secure the service). Device storage may hold preferences and short-lived match cache. Optional Google sign-in (if enabled later) would store name, email, and avatar from Google.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not run a betting wallet or accept wagers.

Third parties

Live scores and display rates come from cricket / odds data providers. The app talks to our API hosted on Render; cache may use Upstash Redis.
